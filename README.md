**Annaprasanna** is a premium, privacy-first **Vedic Nutrition Intelligence** application. It transcends traditional calorie counting by focusing on **Ahara** (mindful nourishment). By blending thousands of years of Ayurvedic principles with cutting-edge Google Gemini AI via Genkit, Annaprasanna helps you align your diet with your body's natural constitution (**Prakriti**) and the spiritual qualities of food (**Gunas**).

## 🌟 Premium Features

### 📸 Vedic Food Scanner (AI-Powered)
Instantly identify any meal using your camera. Our Gemini-powered engine goes beyond macros to reveal:
- **Guna Classification:** Is your meal Sattvic (Pure), Rajasic (Stimulating), or Tamasic (Heavy)?
- **Ayurvedic Wisdom:** Personalized tips on how this specific food interacts with your soul and digestive fire (*Agni*).
- **Nutritional Transparency:** Accurate calories and macros derived through visual analysis.

### 🧪 Viruddha Ahara (Compatibility Checker)
Not all healthy foods are healthy *together*. Our AI analyzes food combinations to detect "Viruddha Ahara" (incompatible foods) which, according to Ayurveda, can lead to toxin accumulation (*Ama*).

### 🧘 Mindful Eating Timer
A guided ritual for your meals. Features three stages:
1. **Prarthana (Prayer):** A moment of gratitude before you begin.
2. **Ahara (Nourishment):** Focused, slow, and mindful chewing.
3. **Vishrama (Rest):** A calm period after the meal to start proper digestion.

### 🛡️ Silent & Privacy-First Mode
Focus purely on quality. One-tap **Silent Mode** hides all numerical data like calories and weight, allowing you to connect with the *essence* of your food without the stress of numbers.

### 📊 Spiritual Guna Balance
Track your Sattvic, Rajasic, and Tamasic intake over time. Visualize your mental and physical state through beautifully crafted Recharts visualizations.

## 🛠️ State-of-the-Art Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router, Server Actions)
- **AI Core:** Genkit AI with Google Gemini 1.5 Flash
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with Framer Motion for premium animations
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Visualization:** Recharts (Heatmaps, Radar Charts, Composition Bars)
- **UI Architecture:** Radix UI Primitives & Shadcn UI

## 🚀 Getting Started

Ensure you have [Node.js 18+](https://nodejs.org/) installed.

1.  **Clone & Install:**
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/sourishdey2005/Annaprasanna.git
    cd Annaprasanna
    npm install
    ```

2.  **Environment Setup:**
    Create a `.env` file in the root based on `.env.example`:
    ```env
    GOOGLE_GENAI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
    ```
    *Get your key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).*

3.  **Run Development Server:**
    ```bash
    npm run dev
    ```
    Visit `http://localhost:3000` to see the experience.

## 📖 The Philosophy

> *"Yatha Annam, Tatha Mannam" — As is the food, so is the mind.*

In the Vedic tradition, food is the primary medicine. **Annaprasanna** (named after the sacred ritual of a child's first solid food) aims to re-introduce that sacredness into our daily modern lives.
